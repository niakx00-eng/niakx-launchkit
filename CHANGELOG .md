## Versioning Notes

NIAKX LaunchKit follows semantic versioning (`MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`):

- **Major** (`x.0.0`) — a brand system change: new logo direction, color palette revision, or a full documentation restructure. May require re-familiarizing with usage rules.
- **Minor** (`1.x.0`) — additive changes: new prompt categories, new landing page variants, new deck sections, or expanded guidance that don't alter existing brand rules.
- **Patch** (`1.0.x`) — corrections: typo fixes, broken link repairs, or clarifications that don't change guidance or assets.

All updates are included at no additional cost for the lifetime of the product. See [`LICENSE.txt`](./LICENSE.txt) for full terms.
