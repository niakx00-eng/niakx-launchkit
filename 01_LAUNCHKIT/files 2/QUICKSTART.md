# NIAKX LaunchKit — Quick Start Guide

This guide walks you through your first full cycle in LaunchKit: from a blank brief to a published landing page, brand system, and pitch deck. Budget about 15 minutes for your first pass — most of that is thinking through your answers to the brief, not waiting on generation.

## Before You Start

Have these on hand:

- A one-sentence description of what you're building and for whom.
- Your best guess at 2–3 direct or indirect competitors.
- Any existing brand material (a name, a tagline, colors you already like) — optional, but it saves a step.

You don't need a finished pitch or validated messaging. LaunchKit is designed to produce a strong first draft from a rough input; refinement happens after generation, not before.

## Step 1 — Create Your Brief

Start a new project and answer the structured intake:

1. **What are you building?** One or two sentences, plainly stated. Avoid marketing language here — describe the mechanism, not the pitch.
2. **Who is it for?** Name a specific persona, not a broad market. "Solo SaaS founders pre-seed" beats "small businesses."
3. **What does success look like in 90 days?** This shapes the tone of your deck and site copy more than any other answer.
4. **Competitors or alternatives.** Include "doing nothing" or "building it yourself" if those are realistic alternatives — they usually are, early on.

The more concrete this brief is, the less editing you'll do later. Vague inputs produce generic drafts; specific inputs produce usable ones.

## Step 2 — Generate Your System

Submit the brief. LaunchKit generates four things in parallel:

- A brand direction (logo concept, palette, typography, voice)
- A positioning summary and one-page narrative
- A first-draft pitch deck (cover through ask)
- Landing page copy and layout

Generation typically takes a few minutes. You'll see each piece arrive as it completes rather than waiting for the full batch.

## Step 3 — Refine

Review each output and either:

- **Edit inline** — change specific lines, colors, or slides directly.
- **Redirect with a note** — leave a comment like "make the tone more technical" or "the palette feels too playful for a fintech audience," and regenerate just that piece.

Because every asset traces back to the same brief, refining your positioning statement will prompt LaunchKit to flag the deck and site copy that were built from the old version, so nothing quietly falls out of sync.

## Step 4 — Publish and Export

When you're satisfied:

- **Publish** your landing page with one click — it comes with basic analytics enabled by default.
- **Export** your pitch deck as a `.pptx` or PDF for investor or partner sharing.
- **Download** your brand assets (logo files, color palette, type specimens) for use outside LaunchKit.

## What to Do Next

- Invite a co-founder or contractor to your workspace so edits stay in one place (Growth tier and above).
- Set up your referral link if you're on a paid plan — every published site can carry attribution.
- Revisit your brief in 30 days. As your positioning sharpens from real customer conversations, regenerating from an updated brief is faster than manually reconciling four separate documents.

## Common First-Session Questions

**Can I bring my own logo instead of generating one?**
Yes — upload an existing mark during the Brand Studio step and LaunchKit will build the palette and typography system around it instead of proposing a new one.

**What if I don't like any of the generated directions?**
Regenerate with a more specific note rather than a vaguer one. "Try a version with more contrast and less rounded geometry" produces a more useful second draft than "I don't like this."

**Can I use LaunchKit for a second, unrelated venture?**
Yes — each brief lives in its own project. Starter includes one active brand; Growth and above support multiple.
