# NIAKX LaunchKit

**Build. Launch. Scale.**
AI-powered digital products for modern founders.

NIAKX LaunchKit is the startup launch system that takes a founder from a raw idea to a live, on-brand product presence — brand identity, pitch deck, website copy, and go-to-market assets — generated together, from a single brief, and kept consistent as they evolve.

---

## What LaunchKit Does

Founders typically assemble a brand identity, a pitch deck, a landing page, and a go-to-market plan as four disconnected projects, usually with four different tools or freelancers. LaunchKit treats them as one system:

- **Brand Studio** — generates a logo direction, color palette, typography system, and voice guide from a structured brief.
- **Deck Builder** — produces an investor-ready pitch deck, automatically aligned to the brand system above.
- **Copy Engine** — writes website, email, and social copy in a consistent voice, tuned to your audience.
- **Market Radar** — generates market sizing, competitor mapping, and positioning analysis to ground the rest of the system in reality.
- **Site Publisher** — turns the brand and copy into a live landing page with analytics built in.

Every output shares the same underlying brief, so a change to positioning propagates across the deck, the site, and the brand voice instead of requiring four separate edits.

## Who It's For

- **Solo founders** who need a credible brand and site before their first demo call.
- **Startup studios** launching multiple ventures per year and needing a repeatable process.
- **Agencies and consultancies** who white-label LaunchKit to deliver launch packages faster than an in-house team.
